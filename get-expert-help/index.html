FAQ

Is the audit really free?

Yes. The initial review and recommendations are free with no obligation. If you choose to work with a specialist, you'll get a clear quote before anything starts.

Who are the specialists?

Independent developers, IT, cloud and security professionals we vet for experience, communication and references. You'll see their background before agreeing to anything.

How fast will I hear back?

Within one business day, usually sooner.

It depends on scope. Many tasks are fixed-price; ongoing support is usually monthly. You approve every cost up front.
