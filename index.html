Frequently asked questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising, sponsorships and voluntary donations.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Nearly every tool runs entirely in your browser. The only exceptions are the IP and DNS lookups, which query public services directly from your browser — we don't store results.

Can I suggest a tool?

Absolutely — use the contact form or submit it to the directory. Popular requests get built first.

Can you build something custom for my business?

Yes. Our Get Expert Help service matches you with vetted developers, IT and security specialists. The first consultation is free.
